Group One: Project Ideas Proposal

1.	RamHub: Community Forum, account media posting, the RateMyProfessors-style core for specific classes, community feed, campus tabs, AI mascot assistant, AI parking assistant, AI syllabus assistant.

  1.1.	Existing need or problem - class knowledge disappears when students graduate; campus info is scattered; first-year/transfer/first-gen students hit hardest.
 
  1.2.	Target audience - current FSC undergrads, with advisors/prospective students secondary. 
  
  1.3.	Five epics -
  
    1.3.1.	Accounts & Moderation - Personalized accounts and dedicated moderation will allow students to use the app safely and professionally.
    
    1.3.2.	Course & Professor directories - A faster way to view current course offerings and professor information.
    
    1.3.3.	Advice Threads - Places for people to share resources and tips for one another.
    
    1.3.4.	Community Feed & Campus-info tabs - Direct feed of status updates at Farmingdale. Provides an easier place to access campus info.
    
    1.3.5.	Search, filing cabinet, & AI assistant - Allows students to search for any topics and save their favorite pages. The AI assistant can help with parking, scheduling, and reminders.
    
  1.4.	Technologies - In addition to the tech stack, we will need: HTML, CSS, JavaScript.
  
  1.5.	This is our first choice because we all felt passionate about creating a new place for students to connect with classmates and share advice, collaborate, and keep students' knowledge for future use.
  
2.	QuoteWise: A vendor quote comparison system that automatically collects, organizes, and evaluates price quotes from multiple suppliers or vendors so a business can make an informed decision.

  2.1.	Existing need or problem - Most vendors send quotes as PDFs, emails, or their own spreadsheet formats, and current software cannot automatically parse the data, so it must be done manually. Platforms are primarily built for large-scale procurement and have steep learning curves, clunky UIs, and heavy configuration requirements. This presents a challenge for small or mid-sized purchasing teams. 
    
  2.2.	Target Audience - Small-to-mid-sized business procurement staff.
  
  2.3.	Five epics -
  
    2.3.1.	Vendor & Quote Intake - Handles vendor quotes into the system in a usable, standardized way. Supports uploading quotes in multiple file types or as images, as well as manual entry when no file is available.
    
    2.3.2.	AI-Powered Quote Extraction - Uses AI to automatically extract structured data from unstructured vendor quotes from uploaded files and images.
    
    2.3.3.	Quote Comparison Engine - Transforms extracted or manually entered quotes into a standardized, side-by-side comparison.
    
    2.3.4.	Weighted Scoring & Decision Support - Allows evaluation of vendors on criteria beyond price alone.
    
    2.3.5.	Reporting, Exporting & Audit Trail - Makes comparison results usable outside the tool and trustworthy for compliance purposes. 
    
  2.4.	Technologies - In addition to the tech stack, we will need: Anthropic Claude API, React, django-storages.
  
3.	CourseLens - A syllabus analysis software with AI-assisted schedule planning, smart notifications, and other features to assist college students.

  3.1.	Existing need or problem - Students receive so many due dates and assignments each semester that it becomes difficult to track. 
  
  3.2.	Target Audience - Current FSC students.
  
  3.3.	Five Epics -
  
    3.3.1.	Syllabus Intake & Parsing - Enables students to upload syllabus in any common format and have the system extract key data. 
    
    3.3.2.	AI-Powered Adaptive Study Plan Generation - Using the parsed syllabus data, an AI engine generates a personalized study schedule that allocates prep time based on assignment weight, difficulty, and proximity to deadlines.
    
    3.3.3.	Unified Calendar & Conflict Management - Provide a single master calendar that merges class times, deadlines, and generates study blocks across all courses. 
    
    3.3.4.	Progress Tracker & Notifications - Give students the tools to track completion of study sessions and assignments, receive timely reminders scaled to task importance, and see visual progress indicators (streaks, completion rates, upcoming risk periods). 
    
    3.3.5.	Integrations & Collaboration - Connect the platform to external academic systems (Canvas, Blackboard, Moodle) for direct syllabus/LMS import and enable social features like sharing schedules with study groups or classmates to find overlapping free time. 
    
  3.4.	Technologies - In addition to the tech stack, we will need: django-allauth, Google Calendar API, django-storages.
