Working on campus

Campus jobs come in two kinds: Student Assistant and College Work-Study. Departments that hire students include Admissions, Student Activities, Athletics, the Tutoring Center, Human Resources, the Writing Center, Information Technology, and the Library.

  • Work-study depends on your financial aid; ask the Financial Aid Office (faoffice@farmingdale.edu) whether you qualify.
  • Departments don’t have to post openings on CareerConnect, so it’s worth asking the offices you’d like to work in directly.
  • Before your first shift: finish the new-hire paperwork online and book an I-9 appointment with Human Resources.

Career help at the Nexus Center

The Nexus Center, in Greenley Hall, offers:

  • Career counseling appointments, booked in CareerConnect.
  • Resume and cover letter reviews: email your document to nexuscenter@farmingdale.edu.
  • Online tools: Focus 2 career assessments, Big Interview for interview practice, and Jobscan for resumes.
  • LinkedIn Learning access for students.
