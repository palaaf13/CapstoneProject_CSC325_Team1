Getting a permit

  • Every student who drives on campus has to register the car with University Police and get a permit.
  • Apply and pay online at {% include "campus/partials/official_link.html" with url="https://farmingdale.t2hosted.com/Account/Portal" label="Student Parking Permits" %}. The fee is set by SUNY and shown when you apply.
  • Stick the decal on the top of the rear side window, driver’s side, no later than 48 hours after the first day of classes. Tape doesn’t count as attached.
  • A permit can’t be moved to another car or another person.
{% include "campus/partials/source_note.html" with url="https://www.farmingdale.edu/university-police/parking-and-traffic.shtml" label="Parking and Traffic" %}

Where you can park

  • White lines mark student parking. Blue spaces are accessible parking and need an accessible permit.
  • Only marked spaces count: no parking along curbs or roadways.
  • Overnight parking (midnight to 6 a.m.) is for residence hall students only.
  • Visitors need a temporary permit from University Police.

Find the lots on the {% include "campus/partials/official_link.html" with url="https://map.farmingdale.edu" label="campus map" %}. See also {% include "campus/partials/official_link.html" with url="https://www.farmingdale.edu/university-police/accessible-parking-information.shtml" label="accessible parking information" %}.

  • You have 5 business days to pay or appeal. Miss the window and the ticket stands.
  • Pay online through {% include "campus/partials/official_link.html" with url="https://farmingdale.t2hosted.com/Account/Portal" label="Pay Parking Citation" %}, or appeal through {% include "campus/partials/official_link.html" with url="https://www.farmingdale.edu/university-police/parking-appeals.shtml" label="Parking Ticket Appeal" %}.
  • Unpaid fines block class registration, and grades and transcripts are held until they’re paid.
  • Ten or more violations in one academic year can cost you your parking privileges for the rest of it.
  • Towed or booted? Go to the University Police office.
